Privacy Policy

Effective date: June 4, 2025  ·  Last updated: June 4, 2025

Overview

This Privacy Policy describes how the WHOOP Dashboard ("the App") handles information when you connect your WHOOP account. We collect only what is necessary to display your health data and never share it with third parties.

Data We Access

When you authorize the App via WHOOP's OAuth 2.0 flow, we request read-only access to:

  • Recovery score and heart-rate variability (HRV)
  • Daily strain and cycle metrics
  • Sleep performance and duration
  • Recent workout activity
  • Basic profile information (first name, last name)

How We Use Your Data

Your WHOOP data is fetched in real time from WHOOP's API on each page load and rendered directly in your browser. It is used solely to display your personal health metrics on the dashboard.

We do not persist, analyze, aggregate, or share your health data. Nothing is written to a database or sent to any external service.

Session & Token Storage

OAuth tokens issued by WHOOP are stored in a server-side session for the duration of your browser session. Sessions are invalidated when you click "Disconnect" or close your browser. Tokens are never written to disk or included in logs.

Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or transfer your personal information to any outside parties. Your data moves only between your browser, this application server, and WHOOP's official API.

Third-Party Services

The App communicates exclusively with the WHOOP API (api.prod.whoop.com). No analytics, advertising, or tracking scripts are loaded.

Data Retention

No health data is retained after your session ends. Disconnecting your account immediately destroys the server-side session and all associated tokens.

Your Rights

You may revoke this application's access to your WHOOP data at any time by:

  • Clicking "Disconnect" in the dashboard header
  • Removing the App's authorization in your WHOOP account settings at app.whoop.com

Security

All communication with WHOOP's API is performed over HTTPS. OAuth tokens are kept in server-side sessions only and are never exposed to the client. We follow WHOOP's API security guidelines and do not persist credentials.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Changes will be reflected by an updated effective date at the top of this page.
